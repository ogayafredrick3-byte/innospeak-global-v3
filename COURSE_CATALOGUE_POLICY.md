# InnoSpeak Global course catalogue policy

## Publication, learning access and academic status are separate

The project owner has authorised all 368 generated catalogue curricula for publication and self-paced online study. The catalogue and LMS should not display obsolete generic messages such as “Academic approval required” or “Next intake to be confirmed” solely because a live cohort schedule has not been configured.

This owner authorisation does not imply independent academic validation, external accreditation, professional licensing, a confirmed instructor, specialist equipment availability or a scheduled instructor-led cohort. Those are separate records and claims.

## Course and offering states

- **Catalogue published:** the course can be discovered and its content displayed.
- **Owner-authorised self-paced study:** a learner may enrol in a published course through the supported free-enrolment or verified paid-checkout flow.
- **Independent academic review:** a separate governance record; never infer it from owner authorisation or the presence of generated content.
- **External accreditation or qualification recognition:** only claim it when verified with the relevant awarding or regulatory body.
- **Live cohort or instructor-led offering:** must have its own schedule, instructor, delivery arrangements, resources and any necessary safety checks. It does not block self-paced study by default.

## Curriculum standard

Each course should have a course-specific description, observable outcomes, sequenced modules, guided practice, evidence of learning, an applied capstone, assessment criteria and a 100% assessment weighting. The current generated curriculum provides a structured baseline for all 368 courses; it is not a substitute for qualified subject-expert review where the subject, delivery or claims require it.

Technical, engineering, health, laboratory and other safety-sensitive activities require appropriate competence, supervision, equipment, risk assessment and safe operating procedures before any physical practical delivery is advertised.

## Price and enrolment rules

- The browser may display catalogue pricing but must not be the authority for a payment amount.
- Free published courses can use the self-enrolment policy.
- Paid courses must be enrolled through a verified payment flow; a learner must not be able to insert an active enrolment directly through the browser.
- Payment confirmation is server-side only and must be linked to a verified provider transaction.
- Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led programmes, and regulated delivery arrangements remain separate offerings.

## Governance and review

Keep stable course codes and version history. Record reviewer, approver, review date, decision, evidence, content changes and next review date for formal academic review. Public credential wording must distinguish completion records from externally accredited qualifications.
