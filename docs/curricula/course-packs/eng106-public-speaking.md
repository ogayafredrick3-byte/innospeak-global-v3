# ENG106 — Public Speaking

**Pathway:** English & Communication Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Language and communication

## Publication status

## Course overview

Public Speaking is a transformative course that helps you master the art of persuasive, structured public speaking and presentation. Whether you are addressing a small team or a large audience, you will learn to speak with clarity, confidence, and impact.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English speaking ability
- Willingness to practise in front of others
- Internet access for online learners
- Commitment to weekly presentations

## Learning outcomes

- Use key vocabulary, structures and conventions relevant to Public Speaking accurately.
- Interpret spoken, written or contextual information related to Public Speaking.
- Produce a clear spoken or written response for an identified audience and purpose.
- Improve performance using feedback, self-correction and explicit success criteria.
- Communicate respectfully across contexts while considering tone, inclusion and cultural differences in Public Speaking.
