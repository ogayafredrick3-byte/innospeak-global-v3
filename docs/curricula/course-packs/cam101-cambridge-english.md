**Pathway:** International Qualifications Pathway

## Course overview

Cambridge English prepares you for Cambridge English qualifications, including B2 First and C1 Advanced. You will develop the reading, writing, listening, and speaking skills needed to pass these internationally recognised exams.

### Intended learners

Students and professionals seeking Cambridge English certification for study, work, or visa purposes.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Upper-intermediate English proficiency
- Familiarity with exam formats
- Internet access for online learners
- Commitment to regular practice

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the purpose, key concepts and vocabulary of Cambridge English.
- Apply suitable methods and tools to a realistic task related to Cambridge English.
- Produce a practical work sample that demonstrates the intended Cambridge English competency.
- Evaluate the work against explicit criteria and recommend evidence-based improvements.
- Apply responsible, inclusive, safe and ethical practice relevant to Cambridge English.

Solve a realistic Cambridge English task by defining the need, selecting a method, producing a practical deliverable, checking it against clear criteria and documenting decisions, limitations and improvements.

### Acceptance criteria

- Evidence demonstrates the course learning outcomes and is traceable.
- The work is checked against the stated rubric and success criteria.
- Relevant safety, ethics, privacy, accessibility and attribution requirements are addressed.

- 3. Produce a practical work sample or artefact that demonstrates a relevant Cambridge English competency.
- 4. Evaluate your result against explicit success criteria, identify a limitation and explain a correction or improvement.

- 6. Reflect on feedback and explain what you would improve in a second iteration of the Cambridge English work.
