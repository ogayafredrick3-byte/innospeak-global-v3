# VAS101 — Virtual Assistant Training

**Pathway:** Freelancing & Remote Work Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Career and workplace skills

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Virtual Assistant Training covers calendar management, email handling, customer communication, basic bookkeeping and productivity tools used by remote administrative professionals worldwide.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English proficiency
- Basic computer literacy
- Access to a computer with internet
- Commitment to weekly practice

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the principal concepts, terminology and rules relevant to Virtual Assistant Training.
- Use suitable calculations, records or analytical tools to evaluate a realistic Virtual Assistant Training scenario.
- Prepare a clear financial or business work product with traceable assumptions.
- Check calculations, reconcile results and explain uncertainty or limitations.
- Apply ethical conduct, confidentiality and relevant compliance considerations in Virtual Assistant Training.
