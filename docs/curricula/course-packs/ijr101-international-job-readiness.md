# IJR101 — International Job Readiness

**Pathway:** Career & Global Opportunities Pathway  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks

## Course overview

International Job Readiness covers international CV writing, job search platforms, work visa requirements, cultural preparation and interview techniques for international employment.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Intermediate English proficiency
- Access to a computer with internet

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the purpose, key concepts and vocabulary of International Job Readiness.
- Apply suitable methods and tools to a realistic task related to International Job Readiness.
- Produce a practical work sample that demonstrates the intended International Job Readiness competency.
- Evaluate the work against explicit criteria and recommend evidence-based improvements.
- Apply responsible, inclusive, safe and ethical practice relevant to International Job Readiness.
