# FIN101 — Financial Literacy

**Pathway:** Business & Entrepreneurship Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Finance and accounting

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Financial Literacy covers budgeting, saving, investing, debt management, insurance and financial planning for personal financial health and basic business finance.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English literacy
- Basic numeracy skills
- Access to a computer with internet
- Commitment to weekly assignments

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the principal concepts, terminology and rules relevant to Financial Literacy.
- Use suitable calculations, records or analytical tools to evaluate a realistic Financial Literacy scenario.
- Prepare a clear financial or business work product with traceable assumptions.
- Check calculations, reconcile results and explain uncertainty or limitations.
- Apply ethical conduct, confidentiality and relevant compliance considerations in Financial Literacy.
