## Course overview

CBET Assessor Certification Preparation covers assessment design, evidence gathering, RPL (Recognition of Prior Learning), moderation, feedback and reporting aligned with Kenya TVET assessment standards.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- TVET or assessment background
- Basic English literacy
- Commitment to practical assessments
