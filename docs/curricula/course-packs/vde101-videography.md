# VDE101 — Videography

**Pathway:** Creative Design & Media Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Creative design and media

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Videography covers camera operation, composition, lighting, audio recording and shooting techniques for creating professional video content with any camera.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Access to a camera (smartphone or dedicated camera)
- Basic English literacy
- Commitment to weekly practice

## Learning outcomes

- Identify the principles, conventions and tools used in Videography.
- Analyse references and translate a brief into clear creative requirements.
- Produce an original Videography artefact using an intentional process and appropriate tools.
- Revise the work using critique, audience needs and explicit quality criteria.
- Apply attribution, consent, accessibility and responsible-use practices in Videography.
