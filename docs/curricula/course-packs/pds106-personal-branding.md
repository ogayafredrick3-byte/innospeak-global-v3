# PDS106 — Personal Branding

**Pathway:** Personal & Life Skills Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 4 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Creative design and media

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Personal Branding covers defining your brand identity, online presence, content strategy, professional image, networking and communicating your unique value for career and business opportunities.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English literacy
- Interest in personal growth
- Access to a computer with internet

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the purpose, key concepts and vocabulary of Personal Branding.
- Apply suitable methods and tools to a realistic task related to Personal Branding.
- Produce a practical work sample that demonstrates the intended Personal Branding competency.
- Evaluate the work against explicit criteria and recommend evidence-based improvements.
- Apply responsible, inclusive, safe and ethical practice relevant to Personal Branding.
