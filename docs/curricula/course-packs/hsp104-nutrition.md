**Pathway:** Health, Hospitality & Community Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Nutrition covers macronutrients, micronutrients, balanced diet planning, nutrition across life stages, dietary requirements and community nutrition for personal wellbeing and professional practice.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English literacy
- Interest in health and nutrition
- Access to a computer with internet

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the key scientific concepts and terminology underlying Nutrition.
- Apply an appropriate method to investigate or interpret a defined Nutrition question.
- Produce a clear record, analysis or practical report using relevant evidence.
- Interpret findings, identify uncertainty and distinguish evidence from assumption.
- Follow relevant safety, ethics, privacy and professional boundaries in Nutrition.
