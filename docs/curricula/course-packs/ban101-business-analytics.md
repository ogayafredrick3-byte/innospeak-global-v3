# BAN101 — Business Analytics

**Pathway:** Business & Entrepreneurship Pathway  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Catalogue duration:** 10 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Data and artificial intelligence

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Business Analytics teaches learners to interpret business data, build simple dashboards and reports, and use analytics to guide decision-making across sales, operations and marketing functions.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic computer literacy
- Basic numeracy skills
- Access to a computer with internet
- Commitment to weekly assignments

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core frameworks and terminology used in Business Analytics.
- Analyse a realistic organisational or customer problem using appropriate Business Analytics methods.
- Produce a practical plan, decision brief, campaign or other deliverable relevant to Business Analytics.
- Evaluate outcomes against measurable criteria and justify recommendations with evidence.
- Communicate decisions responsibly while considering stakeholders, inclusion and ethics in Business Analytics.
