# CUS101 — Customer Service Excellence

**Pathway:** Health, Hospitality & Community Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks

## Course overview

Customer Service Excellence covers communication, problem resolution, customer engagement, service recovery and professional conduct for delivering outstanding customer service.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English proficiency
- Access to a computer with internet
- Commitment to weekly practice

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core frameworks and terminology used in Customer Service Excellence.
- Analyse a realistic organisational or customer problem using appropriate Customer Service Excellence methods.
- Produce a practical plan, decision brief, campaign or other deliverable relevant to Customer Service Excellence.
- Evaluate outcomes against measurable criteria and justify recommendations with evidence.
- Communicate decisions responsibly while considering stakeholders, inclusion and ethics in Customer Service Excellence.
