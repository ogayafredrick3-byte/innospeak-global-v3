# PDS111 — Conflict Resolution & Mediation

**Pathway:** Personal & Life Skills Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 4 Weeks

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Conflict Resolution & Mediation covers negotiation principles, active listening, de-escalation, mediation frameworks, cross-cultural conflict and building sustainable agreements.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English literacy
- Interest in interpersonal skills
- Commitment to weekly practice

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the purpose, key concepts and vocabulary of Conflict Resolution & Mediation.
- Apply suitable methods and tools to a realistic task related to Conflict Resolution & Mediation.
- Produce a practical work sample that demonstrates the intended Conflict Resolution & Mediation competency.
- Evaluate the work against explicit criteria and recommend evidence-based improvements.
- Apply responsible, inclusive, safe and ethical practice relevant to Conflict Resolution & Mediation.
