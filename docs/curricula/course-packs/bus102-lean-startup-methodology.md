# BUS102 — Lean Startup Methodology

**Pathway:** Business & Entrepreneurship Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Research and academic study

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Lean Startup teaches the build-measure-learn loop, minimum viable products, customer development and validated learning, helping entrepreneurs reduce risk and build ventures that solve real problems.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English literacy
- Interest in entrepreneurship
- Commitment to weekly assignments

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core frameworks and terminology used in Lean Startup Methodology.
- Analyse a realistic organisational or customer problem using appropriate Lean Startup Methodology methods.
- Produce a practical plan, decision brief, campaign or other deliverable relevant to Lean Startup Methodology.
- Evaluate outcomes against measurable criteria and justify recommendations with evidence.
- Communicate decisions responsibly while considering stakeholders, inclusion and ethics in Lean Startup Methodology.

- 1. Explain the key concepts or principles relevant to Lean Startup Methodology and support your explanation with a course example.

- 4. Evaluate your result against explicit success criteria, identify a limitation and explain a correction or improvement.
- 5. Identify a relevant safety, ethical, privacy, accessibility, attribution or quality consideration in Lean Startup Methodology and explain a proportionate control.
- 6. Reflect on feedback and explain what you would improve in a second iteration of the Lean Startup Methodology work.

Treat this as an owner-authorised assessment draft; obtain specialist review before formal high-stakes, regulated or safety-critical use.
