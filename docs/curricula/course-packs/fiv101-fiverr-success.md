# FIV101 — Fiverr Success

**Pathway:** Freelancing & Remote Work Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Career and workplace skills

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Fiverr Success teaches learners to create optimised gigs, price services strategically, manage orders and build a strong reputation as a freelancer on Fiverr.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic English proficiency
- A marketable skill to offer
- Access to a computer with internet
- Commitment to weekly practice

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core frameworks and terminology used in Fiverr Success.
- Analyse a realistic organisational or customer problem using appropriate Fiverr Success methods.
- Produce a practical plan, decision brief, campaign or other deliverable relevant to Fiverr Success.
- Evaluate outcomes against measurable criteria and justify recommendations with evidence.
- Communicate decisions responsibly while considering stakeholders, inclusion and ethics in Fiverr Success.
