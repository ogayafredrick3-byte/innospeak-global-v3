# DLP106 — Digital Entrepreneurship

**Pathway:** Digital Skills & Productivity Pathway  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Catalogue duration:** 6 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Business and entrepreneurship

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Digital Entrepreneurship introduces the fundamentals of building an online business, covering digital business models, social media marketing, e-commerce platforms, online payments and digital customer acquisition.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic computer literacy
- Basic English literacy
- Access to a computer with internet
- Interest in entrepreneurship

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core concepts, terminology and architecture used in Digital Entrepreneurship.
- Use appropriate tools, data or programming techniques to complete a defined Digital Entrepreneurship task.
- Create a working digital artefact or analysis that demonstrates Digital Entrepreneurship skills.
- Test, debug or validate the result using reproducible evidence and stated acceptance criteria.
- Apply privacy, security, accessibility, licensing and maintainability practices relevant to Digital Entrepreneurship.
