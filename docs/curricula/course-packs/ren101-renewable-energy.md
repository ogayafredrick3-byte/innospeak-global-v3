# REN101 — Renewable Energy

**Pathway:** Engineering, Automation & Innovation  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Catalogue duration:** 8 Weeks  
**Indicative guided/independent learning hours:** 26  
**Domain:** Electrical, electronics and engineering practice

## Publication status

Owner-authorised for catalogue publication and self-paced enrolment. Independent subject/academic validation remains pending; no external accreditation is claimed. Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated delivery are separate offerings.

## Course overview

Renewable Energy covers solar, wind, hydro and biomass energy systems, including system design, installation, maintenance and policy for the renewable energy sector.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- Basic science background
- Basic English literacy
- Internet access for online learners
- Commitment to weekly assignments

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core principles and terminology used in Renewable Energy.
- Apply appropriate calculations, tools, diagrams or procedures to solve a defined Renewable Energy problem.
- Build or document a practical artefact that demonstrates a relevant Renewable Energy skill.
- Test results against stated specifications, interpret evidence and troubleshoot errors.
- Follow relevant safety, environmental, ethical and documentation requirements when practising Renewable Energy.
