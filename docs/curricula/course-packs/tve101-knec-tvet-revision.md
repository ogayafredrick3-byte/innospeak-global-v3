## Course overview

KNEC TVET Revision provides comprehensive revision for KNEC TVET examinations, covering core trade theory, practical skills and past paper practice across engineering, ICT and business trades.

### Intended learners

Learners interested in this programme who meet the entry requirements.

### Prerequisites / entry requirements

- TVET trainee
- Basic English literacy
- Internet access for online learners
- Commitment to intensive revision

## Learning outcomes

- Explain the core principles and terminology used in KNEC TVET Revision.
- Apply appropriate calculations, tools, diagrams or procedures to solve a defined KNEC TVET Revision problem.
- Build or document a practical artefact that demonstrates a relevant KNEC TVET Revision skill.
- Test results against stated specifications, interpret evidence and troubleshoot errors.
- Follow relevant safety, environmental, ethical and documentation requirements when practising KNEC TVET Revision.
