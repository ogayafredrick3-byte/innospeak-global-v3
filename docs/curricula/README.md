# InnoSpeak Global — Owner-Authorised Curriculum Pack (v2.2)

Generated and synchronized on 19 October 2026 for all **368 catalogue courses**.

## What is included

- `course-curriculum-review-pack.json`: structured course records, course-specific learning outcomes, six sequenced modules, practical activities, evidence of learning, applied capstones, assessment plans, sample assessment prompts, rubric, facilitator guide, learner study guide and governance fields for each course.
- `course-packs/`: 368 individual Markdown packs, one per course, synchronized to the same course-specific outcomes, activities, capstones and assessment prompts.
- `academic-approval-register.csv`: one governance record per course.
- `publication-manifest.json`: publication and review status summary.

## Owner authorization and scope

The project owner has authorised all 368 course curricula for catalogue publication and self-paced online study. The website should not block this self-paced route with a generic academic-approval or unconfirmed-intake message solely because a fixed cohort has not been scheduled.

Owner authorisation is distinct from independent subject-expert validation, external accreditation, professional licensing, confirmed instructor availability and scheduled cohort readiness. The pack does not claim any of those statuses without separate evidence.

## Self-paced enrolment and delivery

Self-paced enrolment is open for published courses through the LMS's supported free-enrolment or verified paid-checkout flow. This does not automatically confirm that every lesson has been reviewed by a specialist, that a physical laboratory or instructor is available, or that a fixed-date cohort is scheduled. Learner support, accessibility, the published fee, actual resources and the lesson experience must still be checked as part of launch verification.

Fixed-date cohorts, instructor-led sessions and regulated or safety-sensitive practical delivery are separate offerings. Before advertising them, confirm instructor assignment, schedule, capacity, equipment/software, support, assessment rules and applicable local requirements. Electrical, engineering, laboratory, health, agriculture and other risk-bearing practical work requires qualified supervision and appropriate safety controls.

## Content standard and limits

The curriculum packs are structured, course-specific learning drafts. They provide a useful teaching sequence and assessment baseline, but current authoritative references, jurisdictional requirements, specialist procedures, formal qualification alignment and high-stakes assessment decisions require appropriate validation before those claims or uses are made. Do not present a course as accredited unless the relevant authority has granted that status.
