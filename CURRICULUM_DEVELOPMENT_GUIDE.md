# InnoSpeak Global — Curriculum Development and Review

## Current coverage

The current source contains 368 generated course curriculum records with six modules each (2,208 modules total). Module outcomes and activities are tailored to course titles and broad subject-area patterns, and each course has an applied capstone and assessment plan. The audit rejects missing course curricula, missing modules, repeated generic module-outcome templates and assessment weightings that do not total 100%.

The project owner has authorised this generated curriculum content for catalogue publication and self-paced online study. The generated records are a structured learning baseline, not proof that 368 syllabuses have been independently researched or validated by subject experts. Content depth, references, current standards, workload, resource availability and specialised assessment design still require appropriate review before making stronger academic or professional claims.

## Curriculum structure

Each course should include:

- A clear description, intended audience, prerequisites and observable learning outcomes.
- Six sequenced modules with course-specific outcomes, learning activities and evidence of learning.
- Practice tasks, revision opportunities, an applied capstone and assessment criteria.
- Assessment components with weightings totalling 100%.
- Accessibility, privacy, ethics, attribution and safety considerations appropriate to the subject.
- Accurate duration, fees, delivery mode, credential wording and learner-support information.

## Review workflow

1. Confirm the learner profile, purpose, scope, prerequisites and level.
2. Check that course outcomes are observable, assessable and aligned with each module.
3. Validate the lesson sequence, examples, references, tools and workload.
4. Check that activities and capstone tasks are feasible for the intended self-paced delivery mode.
5. Review assessment briefs, rubrics, feedback, pass criteria, moderation and reassessment rules.
6. Review accessibility, data protection, safeguarding, safety and responsible-use considerations.
7. Verify pricing, credential claims and any external standards or accreditation statements.
8. Record the review decision, reviewer, version, evidence and date in the authoritative governance system.
9. Keep live cohorts and instructor-led offerings separate from self-paced enrolment, and confirm their schedule/resources before advertising them.

## Safety and regulated content

Engineering, electrical, electronics, health, laboratory, machinery and other safety-sensitive content must not imply that unsupervised physical work is safe. Identify simulations versus physical practice, required supervision, equipment, risk assessments and operating procedures. Regulated qualifications and external exam preparation must not be represented as accredited or officially endorsed without current evidence.

## Important status distinctions

- Owner-authorised catalogue publication: authorised by the platform owner.
- Independent academic validation: a separate review record.
- External accreditation: a separate claim requiring evidence from the relevant authority.
- Self-paced enrolment: open for published catalogue courses through the configured LMS/payment flow.
- Scheduled cohort: separate offering with its own confirmed delivery arrangements.
